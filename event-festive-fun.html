Festive Fun at artsdepot

  • Sat 12 December 2026
  • 12.00 midday–3.00pm
  • Apthorp Gallery, artsdepot

The North Finchley Town Team are joining forces with artsdepot to bring people together on the 12th December 2026 through arts, creativity and community at a free festive themed event. Featuring the N12 Community Choir, local singer/songwriter Sofia Weaver, a performance from Innovation Dance School and a Christmas singalong with the Peatles, there will also be a selection of local craft stalls, so come along to connect with others, meet the Town Team and help us celebrate everything that makes North Finchley special.

The event will run from 12 midday to 3pm and will be held in the Apthorp Gallery on the first floor of artsdepot. It is a free event, and you may purchase refreshments from the artsdepot café.
