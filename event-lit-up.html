LIT/UP: Lighthouse

  • Sat 21 – Sun 22 Nov 2026
  • Sat 7.30pm · Sun 6.00pm
  • Pentland Theatre, artsdepot

Lighthouse is part performance, part light show and part immersive sonic experience from musician and composer Jez Colborne, produced by Mind the Gap. A veteran lighthouse keeper is stationed on a barren rock at sea, where decades pass as automation threatens everything he knows. The dark sonic journey draws on jungle, drum & bass and breakbeat, with projection, film, sculpture and movement.

The show runs for 1 hour 10 minutes, followed by a 90-minute rave. The audience stands throughout. Age guidance is 12+, and children aged 14 and under must be accompanied by an adult. Tickets are from £15 and the show is part of Light + Flight.
