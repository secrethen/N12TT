High Road shopfronts get a seasonal refresh

What's happening

Independent traders along the High Road are joining a shared autumn window trail, giving pedestrians a reason to explore beyond the biggest stores.

Who's involved

The trail is a joint effort between shopfronts near Tally Ho Corner and the Grand Arcade, with each participating trader dressing a window around a seasonal theme.

The detail

Around a dozen shops are taking part this year, from the Grand Arcade's small units to independent stores further along the High Road. Paper trail maps are being made available in participating shops so visitors can follow the route at their own pace, with a short spotlight on each trader's story.

Organisers say the trail is intended to run for the rest of September, with a small closing event planned to coincide with half term. Exact dates for individual shops may vary — check in store for the latest details.
