Light + Flight: Weekend 1

  • Sat 14 – Sun 15 Nov 2026
  • Sat 12.00–9.00pm · Sun 11.00am–7.00pm
  • Finchley Lakeside, Squires Lane, N3 2QT

Light + Flight is Barnet's borough-wide celebration of art, music, performance and heritage, created with and for local people. Weekend 1 takes over Finchley Lakeside, a five-storey complex overlooking a pine-flanked lake that is opening its doors to the public for the very first time.

Expect large-scale art installations, live music and DJs, film and performance, creative workshops for all ages, and food and drink. The weekend also sees the unveiling of a major new artwork, Ascendance, which lights up the atrium at sunset. This is an indoor event and entry is free.
