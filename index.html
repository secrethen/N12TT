What’s coming up

Festive Fun at artsdepot

  • 12 December 2026
  • 12.00–3.00pm
  • Drama Studio
  • Free to all, just turn up

The North Finchley Town Team are joining forces with artsdepot to bring people together through arts, creativity and community at a festive themed event.

Featuring the N12 community choir, Sofia Weaver, Innovation Dance School and The Peatles providing the entertainment.

There will also be a selection of local craft stalls to enjoy.
