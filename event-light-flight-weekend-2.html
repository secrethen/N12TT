Light + Flight: Weekend 2

  • Sat 21 – Sun 22 Nov 2026
  • Sat 10.00am–3.30pm (RAF Museum)
  • RAF Museum London and artsdepot

Weekend 2 of Light + Flight celebrates Barnet's cultural hubs with special programming at the RAF Museum London and artsdepot.

On Saturday 21 November, From Barnet with Love at the RAF Museum (10.00am–3.30pm) offers free activities inspired by Britain's first official airmail flight, which left Hendon Aerodrome in 1911. At artsdepot, the LIT/UP weekend includes the live performance Lighthouse (Saturday evening and Sunday) and a family rave on Sunday 22 November. Festival activities are free, but some artsdepot performances are ticketed.
