Weekly Pub Quiz

  • Every Tuesday
  • 8.00pm
  • The Bohemia, North Finchley, N12

Our pub quiz starts every Tuesday at 8pm at The Bohemia in North Finchley, N12. It can be competitive but mostly it is fun. It is £2.50 to enter and first prize is a £50 bar tab, plus more prizes during the night to keep it interesting. Bring a date or bring some mates!
