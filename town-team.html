Town Team

North Finchley Town Team is focused on building a sustainable community organisation for North Finchley residents, businesses and stakeholders.

Overall aims

Create a coordinated community organisation representing North Finchley residents and businesses.
Improve and promote the town centre.
Develop stronger relationships with local businesses and property owners.
Develop a stronger collective voice for funding and advocacy.
Plan and support events, community engagement and environmental initiatives.

Current organisational work

Develop governance and structure.
Create a website — events, news and calendar updates.
Branding and communications.
Investigating funding opportunities.

Possible events

Ideas being explored for the Town Team's events programme include:

Community events
Networking events
Public engagement events
Involvement of local groups, such as the N12 Community Choir
Event sponsorship opportunities
Town walkabouts
Litter-pick activities
Want to get involved? Come along to a Town Team meet-up — see the next event on the calendar.
